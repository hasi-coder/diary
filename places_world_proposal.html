2 · The world (playable mock)

Full-window in the app (top bar and side nav hidden, Esc exits). Click the floor to walk, click a door to open or close it, click a chair, sofa or toilet to sit, click a person to tell them to follow or wait. Doors open as people pass and close behind them; a toilet cubicle locks while it's in use. The app draws this in smooth low-poly 3D with three.js; this mock draws the same layout flat on a canvas.

Click a tile, a room, or W A S D. Paths go around furniture and through doors (A* on a 0.5 m grid in the app, 1 m here). The camera rotates in 90° steps with Q / E in the app.

Each head is one of four images. Walking toward you shows front, down-left left, down-right right, away back. Watch the heads as people walk.
Room for groups
Cubicles and fitting rooms are 2 × 2 m and lock with everyone inside; aisles and corridors let two people pass. Try the Saree shopping post, or invite Saki and use the family cubicle at the clinic.
Who's there
On entry, people whose home or work it is are already in (Amma, Kavi and Saki at Home; Dr. Fernando and Dilini at the clinic). Opening a post adds its people.

3 · Open a post's place

A post with a check-in and tagged people gets Open place. The place opens with those people already inside, gathered where the visit happened. Save moment in the world adds a post here with a snapshot.

8 · What gets stored

All of it is JSON on existing profiles, encrypted like everything else. No new record types.

City

A place profile, category “City”. Cover photo = the city's picture.
  • Places link to it with relation located-in
  • No layout, no streets

Place layout

Profile.layout replaces plan (old plans dropped)
  • floors[] → rooms, doors, walls, items
  • 0.5 m grid, rotations 0/90/180/270
  • Items from the catalogue, with several seats/use spots each
  • Sized for groups: corridors 1.5 m, cubicles and fitting rooms 2 × 2 m

Person

Existing person / me profiles gain three fields
  • character: age, body shape, build, skin, hair, outfit
  • faces: front, left, right, back (media)
  • places[]: {placeId, role: home|work|school|visits}

Post

Unchanged: check-in place_id + mentions
  • “Open place” spawns the mentioned people
  • “Save moment” = a post with a snapshot

MCP tools

Replace set_place_plan
  • create_place, set_place_layout, add_room, add_items
  • create_city, set_character, set_person_faces

World state

Memory only, reset on each visit
  • Doors, positions, who sits where
  • Residents start at their usual spots
